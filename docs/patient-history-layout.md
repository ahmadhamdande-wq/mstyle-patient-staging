# Patient history and first-visit layout

The patient file retains the original chief complaint first, all history editors, past tests, HSG, laparoscopy reports/attachments, hysteroscopy and genetics. Submitted history balances the visible cards across two equal desktop columns, so a file with findings only in general, menstrual and obstetric history does not leave its right half empty. Cards remain intact and readable; phones use one column. The full-width quick-action strip uses three buttons per row in two rows. The mobile header remains sticky.

**Submit history** saves through the existing patient-file and online-save path. It displays recorded findings and hides empty/non-applicable history groups. It does not create or update an encounter. **Edit history** restores every original history section and its editing controls. **Past tests & reports** reveals the past-investigation sections, including laparoscopy and its attachment controls.

Normal and negative past investigation results are recorded findings. Their dates and reports remain visible; they must not be filtered as absent disease history. No stored clinical values or attachments are deleted by the display filter.

**First visit · Examination & ultrasound** appears after history as a separate section, with Examination and Ultrasound side by side in one desktop row and assessment/plan below. The separate pelvic-examination card is removed; its existing findings and editor are retained inside Examination. Phones stack the two cards. **Save first visit** explicitly links these findings to the first-visit encounter summary, including C/C, vitals, examination, ultrasound/attachments, diagnosis and plan. Repeated saves and a correction to the first-visit date update that same encounter, preserving unrelated encounters. Independent edits made in the encounter cannot be overwritten by stale patient-file values. The chief complaint is not automatically marked confirmed. Simply viewing or submitting history never creates a visit.

Pregnancy-chart and procedure-request cards remain directly accessible below this section. The original final-report action is retained as **Preview final report** alongside Save first visit and View first-visit encounter, replacing the large final-report card. ANC keeps its existing chart, date/visit links, reports and original validated save path; this correction does not change ANC data or controls. Duplicate FHR entry stays consolidated under ultrasound, with legacy values retained.

Verification uses fictional records, normal/negative past-test regression checks, separate history-submit and first-visit save checks, type checking, production build and existing clinical regressions. Publish through the owner-controlled manual Cloudflare deployment workflow on main. No database migration is required.
